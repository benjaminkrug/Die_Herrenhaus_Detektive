# Illustrationen – Prompts für ChatGPT (DALL-E)

## Stil-Vorgabe (bei JEDEM Prompt voranstellen)

> **Stil-Prefix (immer zuerst einfügen):**
> "Black and white pen-and-ink illustration for a German children's detective book, target age 8-10. Style: detailed crosshatching, atmospheric shadows, slightly spooky but not scary. Similar to classic 'Die drei Fragezeichen' or 'Emil und die Detektive' interior illustrations. No color. No manga. No cartoon. Realistic proportions for children aged 10. Consistent art style across all illustrations."

## Illustration 1: Dorfkarte von Eichenhain

**Wo im Buch:** Innenseite des Einbands oder erste Seite

**Prompt:**
```
[Stil-Prefix einfügen]

A hand-drawn bird's-eye-view map of a small German village called "Eichenhain". The map should look old and hand-drawn, like a treasure map, with a slightly worn parchment texture.

Elements to include, with labels in German handwriting font:
- Center: "Marktplatz" with a circular stone fountain ("Brunnen") and a massive 300-year-old oak tree ("Die alte Eiche")
- North of the square: A small shop with a green awning, labeled "Meiers Laden"
- East: A small yellow building labeled "Rathaus" (town hall)
- South: A narrow house with green shutters at the end of the village street, labeled "Jonas' Haus"
- West: A church with a tower, labeled "Kirche", with a small house nearby with flower boxes labeled "Frau Bergmanns Haus"
- A dirt path leading uphill from the village to the northeast
- On top of a hill: A large, dark, three-story manor house with two chimneys (one broken), ivy climbing halfway up the walls, a hole in the roof, and an iron fence around it. Labeled "Das Herrenhaus"
- Northwest, at the edge of a forest: A half-collapsed old mill ruin next to a stream, labeled "Alte Mühle"
- A stream ("Bach") flowing from behind the mill through the village
- Fields and trees surrounding the village
- A small forest on the horizon
- A compass rose in one corner
- Title at the top in decorative handwriting: "EICHENHAIN"
- The overall feel should be inviting but mysterious, like a place that holds secrets

Do NOT include: modern buildings, cars, highways, skyscrapers, bright colors, or any text other than the German labels specified.
```

BEN (rear): Slightly hunched because the ceiling is low. His red cap almost touches the stone ceiling. His expression is a mix of fear and reluctant courage. He is muttering to himself. One hand grips the strap of his backpack.

Atmosphere: Claustrophobic but adventurous. The single flashlight creates dramatic light and shadow. The children look small against the ancient stone. This is the deepest they've gone into the mystery.

Do NOT include: monsters, skeletons, spiders (unless very subtle in a corner), magical light sources, a visible end/exit to the tunnel.

## Hinweise zur Nutzung

1. **Stil-Prefix**: Den Stil-Prefix IMMER als ersten Satz einfügen, damit alle 15 Bilder konsistent aussehen.
2. **Reihenfolge**: Am besten erst Illustration 3 (Charaktere) generieren und das Ergebnis als Referenz für alle weiteren Bilder nutzen ("use the same character designs as in this reference image").
3. **Korrekturen**: Wenn ein Bild nicht passt, den Prompt wiederholen mit dem Zusatz "Make the following changes: [spezifische Änderung]".
4. **Format**: Für Buchdruck: Hochformat (portrait), mindestens 2000x3000 Pixel, 300 DPI.
5. **Konsistenz**: Nach dem ersten Bild ChatGPT bitten: "Keep this exact art style for all following illustrations."
